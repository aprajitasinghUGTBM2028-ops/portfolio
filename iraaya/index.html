Our Story

Loud on purpose.

IRAAYA started with two sisters and one refusal — to dress down to fit in. We mix manga line-art, scrapbook chaos and Y2K grids into pieces that feel like the best day of a decade you half-remember.

“Dislike” isn't a threat, it's a filter. If the outfit doesn't make you feel something, it's not ours.
